Free Cloudinary CDN Setup

Cloudinary is 100% Free (No credit card needed). Once configured, your product photos and category banners upload directly to Cloudinary and get instant worldwide CDN links!

How to get your 2 details in 2 minutes:
  1. Go to cloudinary.com and sign up free using your Google/email account.
  2. Copy your Cloud Name shown on your dashboard (e.g. dpqxxxxxx).
  3. Click Settings (⚙️ Gear) → Upload → scroll to "Upload presets" → click "Add upload preset":
    • Set Signing Mode to Unsigned.
    • Copy the Preset Name (e.g. spidey_preset).
    • Click Save.
